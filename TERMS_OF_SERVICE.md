# SusDetector Terms of Service

Effective November 24, 2026

## The hosted bot

AlteredEgos runs and manages this instance of SusDetector. SusDetector is an independent Discord moderation bot. These terms cover the instance operated by AlteredEgos.

SusDetector checks for rapid deletion of Discord’s native welcome messages. Depending on the server’s configuration, it may post a moderation alert, assign a role, and apply a 24-hour timeout. A deletion is a signal for moderators to review. The bot can mistakenly flag a genuine member. Ask your server moderators to review an action if you think it was a mistake.

## Using the bot

Follow your server’s rules, applicable law, and Discord’s [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).
